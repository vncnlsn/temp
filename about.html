Meet the Team

Mark Fischbuch

President & Founder

Mark Fischbuch founded Allstate Permit Services in 1980 after years of hard work across surveying, drilling, permitting, and field operations. He learned the industry firsthand, from the science and technology behind it to the realities of difficult field work. That practical knowledge shaped APS's comprehensive approach to land services and remains at the foundation of the company today.

Zane Nelson

Vice President

Zane Nelson joined APS in 2010, building on a successful career spanning accounting, business analysis, and enterprise operations. He's combined those disciplines to lead the company's day-to-day operations, transform its financial and operational structure, and expand its capabilities.

Through years of hands-on project and field experience, Zane has developed extensive knowledge of land services while remaining deeply involved in client relationships and field oversight. Today, he continues to strengthen APS's operations and position the company for new opportunities across the industry.

We are APS Land

Our work has spanned hundreds of successful projects in 23 states, plus experience in Alberta and Saskatchewan. Thousands of square miles have been successfully permitted, with extensive right-of-way acquired across energy and infrastructure projects. That work has required direct coordination with thousands of landowners across private, state, federal, and tribal lands. We don’t outsource that communication.

Decades in the field have refined how we operate as we continue advancing our technology, processes, and project management, always focused on what matters most: getting the details right. We take pride in protecting landowners, field crews, the environment, and the projects entrusted to us.

In August 2026, we became APS Land Services. The new name reflects the expertise and capabilities we’ve built while carrying forward the standard we started with: hard work, honesty, and being exceptionally good at what we do.
