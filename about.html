We are APS Land

Setting the Standard Since 1980

APS began in 1980 as Allstate Permit Services. From its roots in permitting, the company has since grown to support energy, exploration, and infrastructure projects across North America.

Our work has spanned hundreds of successful projects in 23 states, with additional project experience in Alberta and Saskatchewan. Thousands of square miles have been successfully permitted. We don't outsource communication; our team has coordinated directly with thousands of landowners across private, state, federal, and tribal lands. Decades of experience have refined our approach as we continue to advance our technology, processes, and project management capabilities without losing sight of what matters most: getting the details right. We take great pride in protecting landowners, field crews, the environment, and the projects we're entrusted with.

In August 2026, we became APS Land Services. The new name reflects the depth of expertise and range of capabilities we've built through decades in the field while carrying forward the standard we began with: hard work, honesty, and being exceptionally good at what we do.

Meet the Team

Mark Fischbuch

President & Founder

Mark Fischbuch founded Allstate Permit Services in 1980 after years of hard work across surveying, drilling, permitting, and field operations. He learned the industry firsthand, from the science and technology behind it to the realities of difficult field work. That practical knowledge shaped APS's comprehensive approach to land services and remains at the foundation of the company today.

Zane Nelson

Vice President

Zane Nelson joined APS in 2010, building on a successful career spanning accounting, business analysis, and enterprise operations. He's combined those disciplines to lead the company's day-to-day operations, transform its financial and operational structure, and expand its capabilities. He has developed extensive knowledge of land services through years of hands-on project and field experience. Zane remains deeply involved in strengthening client relationships, personally overseeing field work, and positioning APS for new opportunities across the industry.

You Stay in Control of Your Land

If we've reached out, it's because a project may involve your property. Here's what that means for you.

You Keep Your Land

An access agreement never transfers ownership of your property.

Your Minerals Stay Yours

This isn't a mineral lease. Seismic survey access doesn't touch your mineral rights.

Surveying, Not Fracking

Seismic surveying maps what's underground. It doesn't extract anything.

Clear From Day One

Access, compensation, and timing are all spelled out before any work starts.

We'll always tell you who we represent, why we've reached out, and what to expect before anything happens on your land.

This applies to seismic survey work as well as right-of-way, utility, and other infrastructure projects. The specifics vary by project, and we'll walk you through them directly. No surprises, no fine print.
