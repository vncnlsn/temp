We are APS Land

Setting the Standard Since 1980

From its roots in permitting, the company has grown to support energy, exploration, and infrastructure projects across North America.

Our work has spanned hundreds of successful projects in 28 states, plus experience in Alberta and Saskatchewan. Thousands of successful negotiations have been completed across energy and infrastructure projects. That work has required direct coordination with thousands of landowners across private, state, federal, and tribal lands. We don’t outsource that communication.

Decades in the field have refined how we operate as we continue advancing our technology, processes, and project management.

Always focused on what matters most: getting the details right.

We take pride in protecting landowners, field crews, the environment, and the projects entrusted to us.

Meet the Team

Mark Fischbuch

President & Founder

Mark Fischbuch began working in the industry in 1980, after years of hard work across surveying, drilling, permitting, and field operations. He learned the industry firsthand, from the science and technology behind it to the realities of difficult field work. That practical knowledge shaped APS’s comprehensive approach to land services and remains at the foundation of the company today.

Zane Nelson

Vice President

Zane Nelson joined APS in 2010, building on a successful career spanning accounting, business analysis, and enterprise operations. He’s combined those disciplines to lead the company’s day-to-day operations, transform its financial and operational structure, and expand its capabilities. He has developed extensive knowledge of land services through years of hands-on project and field experience.

Zane remains deeply involved in strengthening client relationships, personally overseeing field work, and positioning APS for new opportunities across the industry.
