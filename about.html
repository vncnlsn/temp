Our Story & Leadership

About APS Land Services

Our Company

Setting the Standard Since 1980

APS began in 1980 as Allstate Permit Services. From its roots in permitting, the company has since grown to support energy, exploration, and infrastructure projects across North America.

Our work has spanned hundreds of successful projects in 23 states, with additional project experience in Alberta and Saskatchewan. Thousands of square miles have been successfully permitted. We don't outsource communication; our team has coordinated directly with thousands of landowners across private, state, federal, and tribal lands. Decades of experience have refined our approach as we continue to advance our technology, processes, and project management capabilities without losing sight of what matters most: getting the details right. We take great pride in protecting landowners, field crews, the environment, and the projects we're entrusted with.

In August 2026, we became APS Land Services. The new name reflects the depth of expertise and range of capabilities we've built through decades in the field while carrying forward the standard we began with: hard work, honesty, and being exceptionally good at what we do.

Landowner Information

You Stay in Control of Your Land

If APS has contacted you, we're representing a company whose project may involve your property. We'll tell you who we represent, why we've contacted you, what is being requested, and what work may take place.

Not a Mineral Lease

Seismic survey access does not transfer your mineral rights.

Not Drilling or Fracking

Seismic surveying maps the subsurface. It does not extract the resources beneath it.

No Transfer of Property Ownership

A seismic access agreement does not transfer ownership of your property.

Defined Access & Compensation

The agreement defines the permitted activities, access, compensation, and other terms before work begins.

For seismic survey projects, APS secures defined access for crews and equipment used to map the geology beneath the surface. These agreements allow specific survey activities without transferring ownership of your property or mineral rights.


APS also supports right-of-way acquisition and other energy, utility, telecommunications, and infrastructure projects. The rights and agreements involved vary by project. We'll explain exactly what is being requested, why it's needed, and the terms that apply to your property.
